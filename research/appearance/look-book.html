Planets, marks and chase lines

Samples of other ways to draw the arena, to choose between by eye. They use the game's own geometry and palette: planets of radius 0.03 arena sides, the 12 checked colours, the pre-start ring of radius 0.18. The moving samples replay rounds recorded from the game's simulation. Nothing here changes the game.

Orientation without the nose

The nose reaches 1.6 r, past the collision circle, so two noses meet before the planets touch. Most of these stay inside the circle; the visor and the moon sit just outside it. Each sample turns three planets at different rates.

Marks for you, your quarry and your hunter

Today's rings reach 2.7 r around your quarry, so in a scrum they cover other planets. These stay within 1.9 r. In each scene you (3) chase your quarry (4), your hunter (2) closes in, and planet 6 is a bystander. The planets wear the notch, since the nose would poke through the tighter marks; the menu switches it.

Planet looks

Each sample is the pre-start ring of 8, the most a round holds. The palette keeps cycle neighbours apart under colour blindness; photos and ID images can't promise that, so they draw the number in the seat's colour. An ID image follows its player from round to round, while numbers follow the join order.

Chase lines

A line from every planet to its quarry closes the cycle. Before the start it traces the ring; in play it shows who is chasing whom. All eight samples replay the same recorded round of 8 at phone size. Pause on a moment to see which styles still read in a still frame.

    Sources

    Planet photos: NASA, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons. Mercury from MESSENGER, Venus from Mariner 10, Earth from Apollo 17, Mars from the Viking orbiters, Jupiter from Hubble, Saturn from Cassini, Uranus and Neptune from Voyager 2.

    ID images: drawn by this page in the style of MetaMask's Jazzicon, Ethereum's Blockies, GitHub's identicons and Boring Avatars' marble. They are not those libraries' exact output.

    Motion: the 8-planet round (ante 5) and the 5-planet round (ante 4) were recorded tick by tick from src/core/sim with every player steered by dev/steer.ts, each with its own gain and nerve, and replayed at 30 frames a second with interpolation.
